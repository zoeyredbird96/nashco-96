About Nashco Global

Building partnerships and delivering excellence across industries for over 25 years

Our Story

Who We Are

Nashco Global Chemicals is a leading multinational corporation specializing in chemicals, automotive parts, complete manufacturing automation solutions, and international trading services. Founded over 25 years ago, we have grown to become a trusted partner for businesses worldwide.

Our comprehensive approach combines industry expertise with innovative solutions to meet the evolving needs of our clients across diverse sectors.

Our Heritage

From our humble beginnings as a regional chemical supplier, we have expanded our operations to serve over 50 countries worldwide. Our growth has been driven by our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

Today, we are proud to be a multi-sector organization offering integrated solutions across chemicals, automotive, manufacturing, and trading industries.

Global Presence

With offices and distribution centers strategically located around the world, we ensure reliable service and timely delivery to our clients. Our global network enables us to source the best products and provide local support with international standards.

We maintain strong relationships with manufacturers, suppliers, and partners across continents to deliver exceptional value to our customers.
